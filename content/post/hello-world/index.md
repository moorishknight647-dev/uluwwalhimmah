In the name of Allāh, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful

And peace and blessings be upon His Prophet Muḥammad, and all of his family and companions and wives.

## Biography of the Imam

*al-Nuʿmān ibn Thābit ibn Zūṭā ibn Marzubān al-Taymī al-Kūfī*, commonly known as **Abū Ḥanīfa (80–150 AH)** was one of the four great  imāms of Islām and the founder of the Ḥanafī school of Islamic jurisprudence. He was born and raised in Kūfah, where he became known as a jurist and ḥadīth scholar. 

He studied under **Ḥammād ibn Abī Sulaymān** for eighteen years, and among his most famous students were **Abū Yūsuf** and **Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥasan al-Shaybānī**.

## Clarifying the Allegations

**Abū Ḥanīfa** is accused of **tajahhum**, being **weak in hadith**, and **irjāʾ'** and **zandaqah** and **kufr**.

## The True ʿAqīdah and Status of Abū Ḥanīfa

Let us analyze the aqeedah of **Abū Ḥanīfa**, and his status in ḥadīth.

### His Status in Ḥadīth

Regarding his status in ḥadīth, many said he is weak (like the imāms of **Al-Jarḥ Wa-Al-Taʿdīl**, but we will both mention his critics, and the people who praised him in ḥadīth and establish proof that he was not a liar.

#### Imāms Who Praised Him And Authenticated Him & Proof He Didn't Lie

**Abū Dāwūd al-Sijistānī** said:

> "May Allah grant mercy upon Abū Ḥanīfa, (he) was an imām."
>
> **Al-Intiqāʾ p.32** by **Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr**

 **Yaḥyā ibn Maʿīn** said:

> "Abū Ḥanīfa was acceptable in ḥadīth, and he did not tell lies."
>
> **al-Sahm al-Muṣīb fī al-Radd ʻalā al-Khaṭīb p.99** by **Al-Malik al-Muʻaẓẓam ʻĪsā al-Ayyūbī**

He also said:

> "Trustworthy; I have not heard anyone declare him weak."
>
> **Al-Intiqā p.127ʾ** by **Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr**

He also said:

> "Abū Ḥanīfah was trustworthy. He would narrate a ḥadīth only when he had memorized it, and he would not narrate what he had not memorized."
>
> **Siyar Aʿlām al-Nubalā v.6 p.395** by **al-Dhahabī**

**‘Alī ibn al-Madīnī** said:

> "And he is trustworthy; there is nothing wrong with him."
>
> **Jāmiʿ Bayān al-ʿIlm wa-Faḍlihi v.2 p.1082** by **Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr**

#### Imāms Who Weakened Him

**an-Nasāʾī** said:

> "He is not strong in ḥadīth; he made many mistakes and errors despite his limited narrations."
>
> **al-Ḍuʿafāʾ wa-al-Matrūkīn v.3 p.163** by **Ibn al-Jawzī**

**Ibn ʿĀdī** said:

> "Most of what he narrates is erroneous and misread, though he has some sound aḥādīth, but he is not among the scholars of ḥadīth."
>
> **al-Ḍuʿafāʾ wa-al-Matrūkīn v.3 p.163** by **Ibn al-Jawzī**

**al-Bukhārī** said:

> "They abandoned him, his opinions, and his ḥadīth."
>
> **al-Tārīkh al-Kabīr v.8 p.81** by **al-Bukhārī**

**Ibn al-Mubārak** said: 

> "Abū Ḥanīfah was poor in ḥadīth."
>
> **al-Jarḥ wa-al-Taʿdīl v.8 p.444** by **Ibn Abī Ḥātim**

### His Aqeedah

He was a Murj'i al-Fuqah, and he affirmed that the Qur'an is uncreated, and was not a kafir

#### Regarding his Irja'

Abū Ḥanīfa was a Murj'i, but not an extreme Murj'i, he was part of the Murj'i al-Fuqaha, a sect of the Murj'iah that appeared in Kufa, and one of them is even the teacher of Abū Ḥanīfa, Ḥammād ibn Abī Sulaymān, the Murj'i al-Fuqaha was not takfirable, as they still believed that sinners were subject to punishment.

##### Daleel

Abū al-Ḥasan al-Ashʿarī said: The ninth sect of the Murj'iah, Abū Ḥanīfa and his companions.

Al-Bukhārī said: He was a Murjiʾ; they refrained from him, his opinion, and his hadith.

#### Regarding His Tajahhum

Abū Ḥanīfa was not a Jahmi, and he spoke the Qur'an was uncreated.

##### Daleel

ʿAbd Allāh ibn al-Mubārak said: 
By Allah, Abū Ḥanīfa did not die believing in the createdness of the Qur’an, nor did he profess that belief as his religion.

And he said: "Jahm was mentioned in the gathering of Abū Ḥanīfah, so he asked: 'What does he say?' They replied: 'He says: The Qurʾān is created.' Thereupon, Abū Ḥanīfah recited: 'Grave is the word that comes out of their mouths; they speak not except a lie.'

Muhammad bin Sa'id bin Sabiq said: I asked Abu Yusuf, so I said: Did Abū Ḥanīfah say the Qur'an was created? He said: Allah forbid! I do not say that either. So I said: Did he hold the opinion of Jahm? He said: Allah forbid! I do not say that either.

Abu Hanifa said in his wasiyyah: We acknowledge that the Quran is the word of Allah, uncreated, His revelation, and His sending.
